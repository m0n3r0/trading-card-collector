# pokemon-card

Notes, price tracking, and purchase checklists for graded Pokemon cards,
plus a general trading-card handbook (sports cards and other TCGs).

## Contents

- `docs/psa10-guide.md` — the complete guide: what PSA 10 means, population
  reports, grading economics, how prices behave, online and offline buying
  playbooks, authentication and fakes, shipping/insurance/taxes, checklists.
- `tracker/` — own-data price tracker (`tracker.py`, standard library only).
  Log sold comps and listing sightings, get ASCII and standalone-SVG charts,
  and buy-signal alerts against your targets. No third-party APIs. See
  `tracker/README.md`.
- `docs/the-hobby-documentary-notes.md` — notes from the "The Hobby" trading-card
  documentary (YouTube), focused on its Pokemon content and what carries over
  to PSA 10 buying.
- `docs/psa-monopoly-video-notes.md` — notes from a commentary video on PSA's
  parent company owning PSA, BGS and SGC (ownership checked against Wikipedia),
  reholder risk, upcharges and independent price checks.
- `docs/trading-cards/` — trading-card handbook beyond Pokemon: market history
  and structure, grading companies compared, sports cards, MTG/Yu-Gi-Oh!/One
  Piece/Lorcana and other TCGs, sealed product, fakes and scams, valuation and
  selling, glossary, and sources.
