# 8. Glossary

Terms already defined in `../psa10-guide.md` section 18 (slab, cert, pop, comp,
reholder, crack, qualifier, buyer's premium, hammer price, eye appeal,
walk-away price, slab premium, shill bidding) are not repeated.

## General hobby

- **LCS:** local card shop.
- **Rip / ripping wax:** opening sealed packs ("wax" from old wax-paper packs).
- **Break / breaker:** live opening of boxes/cases with spots sold to buyers.
- **PC:** personal collection, cards not for sale.
- **Grail:** the most wanted card in a collection.
- **Raw:** ungraded card.
- **Gem / gem mint:** a 10 grade.
- **Pack fresh:** straight from the pack, never handled.
- **Flip:** buy to resell quickly.
- **Vault:** platform that stores cards and lets owners trade without shipping.
- **Mail day:** arrival of purchased cards.
- **Junk wax:** mass-produced late-1980s to early-1990s cards.

## Sports cards

- **RC:** rookie card.
- **Base / parallel / insert:** standard card / color-finish variant / special subset.
- **Numbered (/99):** serial-stamped print run; **1/1** is unique.
- **SP / SSP:** short print / super short print.
- **Case hit:** card inserted at about one per case.
- **Auto / on-card / sticker auto:** autograph types.
- **Relic / patch / RPA:** game-used material; rookie patch auto.
- **Refractor / Prizm / Chrome:** chromium finishes and brands.
- **Hobby / retail / blaster / hanger / mega:** product channels and formats.
- **1st Bowman:** prospect's first Bowman card (baseball).
- **Young Guns:** Upper Deck hockey rookie subset.

## TCG

- **1st Edition / Unlimited / Shadowless:** print-run variants (Pokemon WOTC era).
- **Holo / reverse holo / full art / alt art:** finish and art variants.
- **SIR / IR:** Special Illustration Rare / Illustration Rare (modern Pokemon).
- **Power Nine:** nine most famous early MTG cards.
- **Reserved List:** MTG cards Wizards has pledged never to reprint.
- **Serialized:** numbered modern TCG card (MTG since 2021, others since).
- **Secret Lair:** MTG direct-to-consumer drops.
- **Ghost / Starlight / Quarter Century Secret Rare:** high Yu-Gi-Oh rarities.
- **OCG / TCG:** Yu-Gi-Oh Asian (Official Card Game) vs Western print lines.
- **Enchanted:** top rarity in Disney Lorcana.
- **Manga rare:** top rarity art cards in the One Piece Card Game.
- **ETB:** Pokemon Elite Trainer Box.
- **Bulk:** commons/uncommons sold by count or weight.
- **Ban list:** cards barred from tournament play; affects value of playables.

## Grading

- **Subgrades:** BGS (and optional others) separate scores for centering,
  corners, edges, surface.
- **Black Label:** BGS card with four 10 subgrades.
- **Pristine 10:** top tier above Gem Mint at BGS, SGC, CGC and TAG.
- **Crossover:** moving a slab from one grader to another.
- **Authentic / A:** genuine but not numerically graded (often altered).
- **OC / ST / PD / OF / MK / MC:** PSA qualifiers (off-center, staining, print
  defect, out of focus, marks, miscut).
